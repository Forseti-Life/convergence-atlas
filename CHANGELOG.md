### Notes

- All numerical scenarios are illustrative thought-experiment inputs, not forecasts.
- Reference cards distinguish empirical anchors and published methods from author-defined assumptions; linked datasets do not imply that defaults have been calibrated.
- The IFRS proxy anchors a plausible standards-diffusion timescale but is not an estimate of AI-governance enforcement.
- Dalio’s Big Cycle supplies candidate relationships, not validated coefficients; independent datasets are required for calibration and backtesting.
- PCA results describe the authored model-design space and must not be interpreted as empirical components, causal effects, or real-world probabilities.
- The current constant-hazard empirical baseline underperforms the no-exit null in all six rolling windows and is retained as a negative benchmark.
- Existing literature and proxies may justify exploratory variable defaults without re-performing original research; causal and predictive claims remain gated.
- COW membership exits are not yet classified transition causes and do not validate the full theory.
- COW MID v5.0 ingestion adds 2,436 disputes, 5,883 participants, 4,774 state-year rows, and explicit zero-event system years through 2014.
- COW Formal Alliances v4.1 ingestion adds 148,258 directed-year records, 9,349 state-year features, and 197 annual network snapshots through 2012.
- The first alliance/MID diagnostic finds only weak first-difference associations and makes no causal claim.
- Cached World Bank Indicators API snapshot with six indicators, 14,322 country-year rows, raw-response checksums, missingness, and 188 unreviewed crosswalk candidates.
- Partial empirical WDI PCA on 1,847 complete cases across 135 countries/economies with early/late loading stability and explicit selection-bias warnings.
- Checksum-locked official WGI 2026 workbook ingestion with 5,696 country-year rows, six dimensions, standard errors, intervals, scores, and source counts.
- Provisional COW–WDI–WGI–MID panel with 3,512 rows across 187 Institutions and 970 complete 12-variable cases.
- L2 event-support gate blocks confirmatory prediction because the common period contains one membership exit versus a 120-event screening requirement.
- Observable non-causal taxonomy for all 49 COW membership exits with zero unreviewed causal labels asserted.
- Exploratory next-year MID-onset benchmark comparing null, size, economic, governance, and full models; full model AUC 0.735 on one held-out split with explicit vintage and target warnings.
