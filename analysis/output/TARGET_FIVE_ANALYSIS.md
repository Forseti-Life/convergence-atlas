# Target-Five Exploration

**Objective:** First calendar year with at most five independent top-level Institutions
**Status:** Seeded stochastic scenario behavior; within-model frequencies, not a forecast

## Boundaries with 225 starting Institutions

- Fast high-intensity preset median: calendar year 2029.
- Longest attainment bounded by the 7026 censoring horizon: calendar year 6065.
- There is no finite unconstrained longest case: qualifying waves can be arbitrarily rare or the target can remain unreachable.

## Preset scope

The high-intensity preset uses the maximum effective mean loss probability (95%). Under beta-binomial wave severity and stochastic arrivals, it is a stress-test preset—not a mathematical lower bound. Its median target year is 2029.

The slow preset draws qualifying-wave arrivals around an 833.33-year mean. Its median target year is 6065 and it reaches five by 7026 in 72.0% of replicates.

## Interaction grids

- Frequency-only changes reach five in 19/25 cases at the default 15% event probability and 5% internal conflict.
- Severity × internal-conflict changes reach five in 36/48 cases, spanning calendar years 2063–5627.
- Frequency changes timing only when the per-wave transition produces continued structural decline; it cannot guarantee decline when fragmentation offsets losses.

Institutional-standard adoption remains outside this structural target. AI architecture diffusion affects T₅ only through the explicit consolidation-acceleration feedback when κ_A is above zero.
