# Worldwide Governance Indicators 2026 — Data Use Decision

**Snapshot:** October 6, 2026  
**Coverage requested:** 1996–2025  
**Status:** Approved for local non-commercial analysis

The WGI provides annual composite indicators for voice and accountability, political stability, government effectiveness, regulatory quality, rule of law, and control of corruption. The World Bank explicitly advises treating WGI values as estimates based on perception data rather than definitive ratings.

## Controls

- API v2 raw JSON is cached and checksumed.
- Aggregates are excluded through World Bank region metadata.
- Historical revisions are disclosed; current snapshots are not treated as contemporaneous vintages.
- WGI dimensions are not interpreted as independent causal effects without a declared design.
- Row-level redistribution remains subject to World Bank dataset terms.

## Sources

- WGI: https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/worldwide-governance-indicators
- World Bank dataset terms: https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets
