# COW Intra-State War v5.1 — Data Use Decision

**Reviewed:** October 7, 2026  
**Coverage:** 1816–2014  
**Status:** Approved for local analysis; redistribution unresolved

The source records civil, regional, and intercommunal wars within state territories and provides coded outcomes and state participants. It is used to estimate descriptive state-membership exit risk following civil-war exposure.

## Critical limits

- Civil war does not imply sovereign dissolution.
- Challenger victory does not automatically imply fragmentation or incorporation.
- COW war outcomes and state-system membership outcomes are separate constructs.
- Missing battle-death codes remain missing and are not converted to zero.
- All links to state exits are descriptive timing associations unless historically reviewed.

## Source

- COW War Data: https://correlatesofwar.org/data-sets/cow-war/
- Requested citation: Sarkees, Meredith Reid and Frank Wayman. 2010. *Resort to War: 1816–2007*.
